Intro

I lead software engineering and AI teams at Paylocity, building intelligent products that reshape how people work.

I'm passionate about working smart, doing what's right, and making the world a better place. I'm also an amateur jazz guitarist — and much like jazz, I'm at my best when I'm adapting to change, finding harmony in chaos, and collaborating with technically skilled and creative people.

PMP, CSM, and CSPO certified, with a Master's in Computer Science and a graduate certificate in Generative AI, I bring a rare blend of engineering leadership, business judgment, and technical depth.

Work

As Manager of Software Engineering and AI at Paylocity (2025–Present), I lead engineering teams building AI-powered products — owning technical strategy, team development, and the delivery of systems that change how work gets done.

Previously, at DoSomething.org (2022–2025), I led engineering and delivery for a natural language-to-SQL application, data visualization platforms (d3, Leaflet), and award-winning digital programs. I drove the architecture and rollout of enterprise systems behind a novel web platform and built the engineering practices that let a small team punch far above its weight.

At Fast Enterprises (2021–2022), I led GovTech software implementations with sub-1% defect rates — owning testing strategy, technical enablement for 400+ users, and the communication pipelines connecting engineering to external stakeholders.

Earlier, at Youth Job Center, I rebuilt program workflows on Salesforce and led the technical integration work that connected partner organizations to shared infrastructure.

The throughline across every role: build engineering teams and systems that scale impact.

Education

M.S. in Computer Science, University of Colorado Boulder — with a graduate certificate in Generative AI. Combined with my undergraduate degree in Organizational Change from Northwestern University, this gives me a rare blend of deep technical knowledge and the organizational instincts to put it to work.
